About EntoFarm

EntoFarm is an innovative agribusiness company specializing in Black Soldier Fly (BSF) farming and sustainable agriculture solutions. We focus on transforming organic waste into high-value insect protein and organic fertilizer, helping farmers access affordable, eco-friendly livestock feed and soil enhancers. At EntoFarm, our mission is to promote circular farming systems that improve productivity, reduce environmental impact, and support a greener future for agriculture.

Our Services

BSF Production & Supply

Providing high-quality BSF larvae as a sustainable, protein-rich feed ingredient for poultry, fish, and pig farmers.

Organic Fertilizer

Producing and selling frass (BSF compost) and other organic soil enhancers to improve crop yields naturally.

Bioconversion Services

Partnering with households, markets, and farms converting organic waste into agricultural inputs, BSF bioconversion.

BSF Farm Setup & Consultancy

Offering design, installation, and advisory services for individuals and organizations interested in starting their own BSF farms and modern organic farming methods

Training & Capacity-Building Workshops

Providing hands-on training, virtual courses, and field demonstrations on BSF farming, circular agriculture, and sustainable waste management.

Research & Product Development Services

Collaborating with universities, NGOs, and agribusinesses to develop improved BSF systems, feed formulations, and sustainable agriculture innovations.
